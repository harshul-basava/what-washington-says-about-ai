# Astra review of missing federal AI overall stances

Review completed on November 9, 2026 (UTC).

Astra reviewed all 33 profiles with at least three verified excerpts but no AI overall stance. The cohort contains 22 officials, 11 institutions, and 466 excerpts. The same congressional rubric and three-excerpt minimum apply.

## Result

Astra changed one profile: Ethan Klein now scores 4, or enthusiastic, for AI overall. His September 17, 2025 statement expresses broad optimism about emerging technologies. The surrounding statement explicitly includes AI. Astra assigned confidence of 3 out of 5 because the statement covers several technologies together. The site preserves that limitation. This confidence score is not a measured probability of correctness.

The remaining 32 profiles still lack a qualifying general stance in this corpus. Their statements can support other scores or specific policy judgments. This result does not establish that they have no personal views about AI.

Astra initially identified a tentative stance for Pete Hegseth. After reviewing source context, it classified his statements as military-specific and withdrew that finding. Both review passes remain in the audit file.

## Source checks and cost

Astra requested context for 22 excerpts across 14 profiles. All 22 received context from public pages, cached source text, a retrieved transcript passage, or the official X API. Every context passage retains its source URL and text hash. The checks require the original excerpt to occur in the retrieved text. X checks also require the original post and author IDs to match. The accepted correction also requires an exact attribution passage.

The official X workflow retrieved 10 posts and cost $0.05. Five supplied context that the earlier retrieval lacked; five replaced cached text with official post text. [The workflow completed successfully](https://github.com/harshul-basava/what-washington-says-about-ai/actions/runs/42566794306).

The OpenRouter cost ledger records $2.989623 for this audit. The combined model and X cost is approximately $3.04. Fourteen initial concurrent model requests failed before inference. Sequential retries completed them. No final review has an error or an unresolved context request.

## Publication and daily behavior

The audit preserves every original model assessment and every original excerpt. It adds a final Astra assessment for AI overall only. A correction requires confidence of at least 3 out of 5. Both effective score inputs use Astra's final label for a correction; they are not independent judgments.

The site shows all 33 profile review explanations. The corrected statement also shows its source context. The correction updates Klein's summary, score, reconstructed history, and score-change record. The other four score categories, regulation positions, topics, and earlier observed snapshots retain their original values. Daily updates retain valid statement corrections and assess new evidence through the existing cascade.

This was a targeted review of the existing corpus and its sources. It was not a new exhaustive historical search. Astra received the original rubric without prior model answers. Context reviews received the retrieved sources without the earlier model conclusions.
