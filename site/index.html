What does Washington say about AI?

For every member of Congress and every newly elected member, we collected what they have said publicly about AI: floor speeches, hearings, posts on X, interviews and press releases. We checked every quote against its source. A language model then rated each person on five questions, citing only those quotes.

Method

  1. Who: all 539 current members (senators, representatives and delegates) from the unitedstates/congress-legislators project, plus winners of the November 2026 elections who aren't already in Congress, taken from called races only.
  2. Official records: AI-related quotes from the Congressional Record and hearings, from CeSIA's Politicians on AI dataset (CC BY 4.0). Only the quote text, date and source are used. CeSIA's own labels are not.
  3. X: each person's posts since January 2023 that match AI keywords (retweets excluded), collected through X's web search. In a check against the official full-archive API, this found about 94% of matching posts.
  4. Web: a language model (Claude Sonnet 5.5) with web search looked for every substantive public statement about AI and was told explicitly that finding nothing is fine. We fetched every source page and kept a quote only if it appears there with a fuzzy match of at least 90%. When a site blocks automated access, we checked against the page text the search engine had retrieved.
  5. Scoring: the model saw only the verified statements for one person (no names of other sources, no party labels and no prior scores) and scored five questions on a written 0–4 rubric, or answered "insufficient evidence". We ran it twice with the statements shuffled. If the two runs differed by 2 or more on any question, the person was flagged for human review; otherwise the two scores were averaged.
  6. Human check: we hand-reviewed the people with the highest scores on AGI and existential risk, a random sample, and everyone who was flagged.

Caveats. What people say in public is not the same as what they believe or how they vote. Scores are produced by a language model and only spot-checked by people. Official records lag by months. X coverage depends on knowing the right accounts, and some members barely post. Members who rarely talk about AI will have little or no evidence. That doesn't mean they hold no views.
